## Placement rules

- Demolition and active-facility photographs are used only for demolition, safety, field-team, and operational stories.
- Construction photographs are used only for construction, preconstruction, framing, and wider JZ Group field-coordination stories.
- Waste Management and Development retain their existing division-specific media because this supplied folder does not contain accurate waste or development photography.
- The new photographs are treated as representative field imagery. They are not attributed to a named past project until JZ confirms the project identity and usage approval.
- Near-duplicate frames remain in the source library for future campaigns but are intentionally excluded from the website to reduce repetition and page weight.
